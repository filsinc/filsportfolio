I'm Fils — a multidisciplinary creator, filmmaker, and on-camera performer based in Brooklyn. I study Interactive Media Arts at NYU Tisch, where I've built everything from AR installations to browser-based games to performance art pieces that live on the street and on the internet.

My creative practice doesn't fit one box. I make short-form content that converts — UGC brand deals, YouTube storytelling, TikTok hooks, and social experiments. I also make music under two artist names, animate an original fictional universe, and co-created a patented hybrid musical instrument.

"I don't just make things that look good. I make things that make people stop scrolling."

What ties it all together: I understand performance — both on-camera and in the data sense. I've grown audiences organically, engineered viral moments, negotiated my own brand deals, and built monetized channels from scratch. I iterate fast, take direction well, and bring original energy to everything I touch.

I've been selected for campaigns with MrBeast and Mark Rober, exhibited AR work at BLANC Museum in Manhattan, and had a documentary film nominated at the New Jersey Film Festival — all before graduating college.
